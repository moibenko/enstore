    In Al9 (kernel 5.*) the change done to st leads to the following problem:
    Tape drives are returning an "Input/output error" when a drive, the host, or

    data loss. However, the driver currently drops these errors even for empty
    drives. This is fixed in kernel 6.* (Al10). Found temporary resolution.
    After first load of st driver (initialization, reboot) rty to rmmmod st, modblrobe st.
    This was proven to work with low statistics, but worked on all reboots.
